## Scope and decisions

- One primary HR owner per job (§6 option 1); assignee notifications use email.
- Similar jobs warn without blocking; recruiters can manage Jobs, viewers only read.
- HR Manager maps to `hr`; interviewer roles cannot manage general Jobs.
- Template upload is the default. Ordinary-document upload is the approved
  extension, with **local rules and zero LLM calls**. Upload never saves a job.
- PDF/DOCX text extraction is supported; DOC remains best-effort. Image-only
  scans require manual entry. File limits follow existing upload settings
  (5 MB default); security checks and isolated parser remain in place.
- Missing and conflicting facts require manual review. Internal identities are
  resolved against active organization users, never inferred from prose.
- Decimal years mean whole months (2.5 = 2 years 6 months); years/months selectors
  make the spec's YY.MM notation unambiguous.
- New dates are UTC instants displayed/entered in organization time. Migration
  004 converts legacy wall-clock dates to equivalent UTC instants, preserving
  their historical organization-local closing boundary exactly once.
- `jobId` is server-generated, globally unique and immutable. New UI job references
  are also generated by the backend; legacy clients retain their reference field.
